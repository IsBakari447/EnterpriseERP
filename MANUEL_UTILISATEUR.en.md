# EnterpriseERP User Manual

This guide explains how to use EnterpriseERP every day. It is written for regular users, managers, sales staff and administrators. You do not need programming knowledge.

## 1. What is EnterpriseERP for?

EnterpriseERP helps a company organize its work in one place.

With EnterpriseERP, you can:

- save customers;
- save suppliers;
- add products;
- follow stock;
- create quotes;
- create invoices;
- record payments;
- follow expenses;
- manage employees and attendance;
- view the dashboard;
- export data;
- keep a history of important actions.

## 2. Before signing in

On the home page, you can:

- read this manual;
- view the main features;
- open the demo;
- download the mobile app if available;
- create an account;
- sign in.

If this is your first time, start with these sections:

1. Sign in
2. Dashboard
3. Customers
4. Products and stock
5. Quotes, invoices and payments

## 3. Sign in

1. Click **Sign in**.
2. Enter your email.
3. Enter your password.
4. Click the sign-in button.

If you do not have an account, click **Create account**.

## 4. Understand the dashboard

The dashboard is the first page after signing in.

It gives a quick view of the company:

- customers;
- available products;
- invoices;
- payments;
- expenses;
- alerts;
- recent activity.

Use it every day to see what needs attention.

## 5. Add a customer

A customer is a person or company that buys from you.

To add a customer:

1. Open **Customers**.
2. Click **Add** or **New customer**.
3. Fill in the name, phone, email and address.
4. Save.

Tip: at least add the name and one contact method.

## 6. Add a supplier

A supplier is a person or company that sells products or services to you.

To add a supplier:

1. Open **Suppliers**.
2. Click **Add**.
3. Fill in name, contact, phone, email or address.
4. Save.

## 7. Add a product

A product is an item or service that your company sells or manages in stock.

To add a product:

1. Open **Products**.
2. Click **Add product**.
3. Enter the product name.
4. Choose the category.
5. Enter the purchase price if you know it.
6. Enter the sale price.
7. Enter the available quantity.
8. Save.

Available categories:

- **EnterpriseERP**
- **Mobile**
- **Cloud**

## 8. Manage stock

Stock shows available quantities.

Use **Stock** to follow incoming and outgoing movements.

Examples:

- stock in: you bought or received products;
- stock out: you sold or used products.

Check stock regularly to avoid shortages.

## 9. Create a quote

A quote is a proposal sent to a customer before a sale.

1. Open **Quotes**.
2. Click **Create**.
3. Choose the customer.
4. Add products or services.
5. Check prices and quantities.
6. Save.
7. Download or print the PDF if needed.

## 10. Create an invoice

An invoice confirms a sale.

1. Open **Invoices**.
2. Click **Create**.
3. Choose the customer.
4. Add products or services.
5. Check the total amount.
6. Save.
7. Print or download the invoice as PDF.

## 11. Record a payment

A payment shows that a customer paid an invoice.

1. Open **Payments**.
2. Click **Add payment**.
3. Choose the invoice.
4. Enter the paid amount.
5. Choose the payment method.
6. Add a reference if you have one.
7. Save.

EnterpriseERP updates the invoice status:

- **Paid** when the invoice is fully paid;
- **Pending** when it is not fully paid.

## 12. Follow expenses

Expenses are money leaving the company.

Examples:

- equipment;
- transport;
- salaries;
- subscriptions;
- administration costs.

To add an expense:

1. Open **Expenses**.
2. Click **Add**.
3. Enter title, category, amount, date and payment method.
4. Save.

## 13. Employees and attendance

Use **Employees** to save people who work in the company.

Use **Attendance** to follow arrivals and departures.

You can:

- add an employee;
- view employees;
- record attendance;
- see who is present today.

## 14. Exports

Exports create files from your data.

They are useful to:

- keep a copy;
- share with an accountant;
- analyze in Excel;
- prepare a report.

Open **Exports** and choose the data to export.

## 15. Security and backups

Good habits:

- use a strong password;
- do not share your account;
- back up data regularly;
- give users the right roles;
- check important actions in the audit.

## 16. Roles and permissions

Roles decide what users can do.

If a user cannot see a menu, check their permissions.

## 17. Mobile app

The mobile app lets you use EnterpriseERP from a phone.

It can help you:

- view customers;
- follow invoices;
- check payments;
- see important data while away from the office.

## 18. Common questions

### I cannot see a menu

Your account may not have permission. Ask an administrator.

### I cannot save

Check required fields. If the problem continues, contact the administrator.

### An invoice stays pending

The invoice may not be fully paid. Check the invoice total and recorded payments.

### Stock is wrong

Check stock movements and correct them if needed.

## 19. Recommended routine

Every day:

- check the dashboard;
- review recent payments;
- check pending invoices;
- add new sales or expenses;
- review stock alerts.

Every week:

- review active customers;
- check stock;
- export important data;
- read reports.

Every month:

- compare revenue and expenses;
- back up data;
- review users and permissions;
- prepare management reports.
